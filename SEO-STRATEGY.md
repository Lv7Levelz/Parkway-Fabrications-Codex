Material and industry pages are deferred until Parkway verifies sufficient first-hand detail. This avoids doorway/thin content. Granulator-screen content should expand through useful specification guidance, identification, measurement and approved case studies—not near-duplicate keyword pages.

## Technical strategy

- One descriptive H1, unique title and description per indexable page.
- Absolute canonical from the approved production `SITE_URL`; preview URLs must never point canonicals at the live domain.
- LocalBusiness/Organization NAP kept in one config source.
- Breadcrumb and Service schema added only when production content and routes are final.
- Crawlable descriptive internal links connect services, project proof and RFQ conversion.
- SVG social artwork is a development asset; supply a photographic 1200×630 production image.
- Search Console, analytics consent and event destination are configured only with client approval.
